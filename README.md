## Wordwalker - My First "Official" Game
<img width="630" height="500" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3b682b79-7a90-4e41-92c3-9bd8c60b64dc" />

Wordwalker is my biggest, most involved project to date. I entered it with a simple goal: to completely develop and publish my very first game, entirely by myself. How hard could it be?...😅

Well, you know what game dev is like. I learned that this project, even with a relatively simple idea, would take a lot of effort. So that's what I gave it.

For 5 months I put my heart and soul into this thing. I'd come home from long nine hour shifts and get right back on the computer, writing code, making art, or reviewing word lists until I fell asleep. I committed most of my weekends to it. I used whatever free time I had at work to plan features and sketch out concepts. It's not an exaggeration to say in that time period, this game became my life. It was immensely fulfilling.

It had to be fulfilling. The most important lesson I learned from all of this is that game development is really hard, and not for the obvious reasons. There is so much more to consider than the big concepts. It's even harder as a solo dev. You're
in charge of it all. Every UI button's function and appearance, every frame of animation and transition, every little bug that might be noticed and criticized by your player. You own ALL of it. And you have to keep going.

Sorry if this all sounds dramatic, but it honestly sums up how I feel about the whole process, having just now finished it. There was a time when it bothered me to no end that I hadn't created something. I did not have a piece of media solely to my own name, one that I was
proud to show to people I knew and say "I made that". Now I have it. And I'm thrilled you're here to see it, too.

I'm ready to make more.
